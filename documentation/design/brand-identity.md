# Your Tax Source - Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Essence
**Core Values:** Trust, Clarity, Approachability, Reliability.
**The "Why":** Taxes are complicated and stressful. We make them simple and stress-free for our Belmont neighbors and beyond.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone
*   **Warm:** Friendly and welcoming, like a conversation with a trusted advisor.
*   **Plain-English:** No "tax-speak" without explanation. We talk like real people.
*   **Helpful:** Always looking for the best outcome for the client.
*   **Empowering:** We don't just "do" your taxes; we help you understand them.

### Voice Examples:
*   **Instead of:** "Please submit your 1099-INT documentation for review."
*   **Try:** "Got any interest from your bank accounts? Just upload your 1099-INT forms here and we'll take care of the rest."

## 3. Color Palette
| Purpose | Color Name | Hex Code | Visual |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Boutique Purple | `#6F4A8E` | Deep, professional purple |
| **Secondary** | Midnight Black | `#1A1A1A` | Modern, high-contrast |
| **Accent** | Lavender Mist | `#E6E6FA` | Soft, complementary |
| **Background** | Clean White | `#FFFFFF` | Minimalist and airy |
| **Text** | Charcoal | `#333333` | High readability |

## 4. Typography
*   **Headings:** *Inter* (Bold/Semi-bold). Clean, modern, and highly legible.
*   **Body:** *Open Sans* (Regular/Light). Friendly and easy to read on screens of all sizes.

## 5. Visual Style
*   **Imagery:** Authentic photos of people, Belmont landmarks, and organized workspaces. Avoid generic "suit and tie" stock photos.
*   **Icons:** Simple, rounded-corner line icons. Not too sharp or clinical.
*   **Spacing:** Generous white space to prevent "tax form fatigue."
