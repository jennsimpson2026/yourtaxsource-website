## 3. Navigation Structure
- **Public:**
    - Home
    - Services (Individual, Business, Advisory)
    - About (The team, The Belmont office)
    - FAQ (Common tax questions)
    - Contact (Form, Map, Phone)
- **Portal (Authenticated):**
    - Dashboard (Overview)
    - Document Center (Upload/Download/Archive)
    - Questionnaire (Intake form for new years)
    - Payments (Helcim integration)
    - Messages (Simple thread with the tax pro)
    - Profile/Settings (MFA setup, Notification preferences)
